# Fernway vendor policy

Synthetic sample for the Stablehand folder review demo. Fernway and every vendor in this folder are invented.

Version 3, approved by the finance lead on 2 March 2026. It applies to every contract with a supplier, courier, software or service vendor, and the other documents in this folder are checked against it.

## 1. Liability

Every vendor contract caps the vendor's liability at no less than the fees paid in the previous 12 months. A lower cap needs the CFO's written approval.

## 2. Data protection

2.1 A vendor that handles customer personal data signs our data processing addendum.

2.2 The vendor tells us about a personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

2.3 The vendor gives us 30 days' written notice before it adds or replaces a sub-processor, and we can object.

2.4 The current sub-processor list is attached to the addendum when it is signed.

## 3. Term, renewal and notice

3.1 An automatic renewal lasts 12 months at most.

3.2 We can stop a renewal with no more than 60 days' notice.

3.3 A vendor that can end the contract for convenience gives us at least 90 days' notice.

## 4. Prices

Price increases above 5% in any 12 months need the CFO's approval before they take effect.

## 5. Insurance

Couriers and anyone working at customers' homes keep public liability insurance of at least 1,000,000 EUR and send a current certificate every year.

## 6. Exclusivity and minimum purchases

Exclusive supply terms and minimum purchase commitments need the CFO's approval.

## 7. Confidentiality

An NDA protects our information for at least 3 years after it ends and has no residuals clause.
