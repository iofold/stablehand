# Tallowpay merchant terms, summary for Fernway

Synthetic sample for the Stablehand folder review demo. Every party in it is invented.

## Fees

1.4% + 0.25 EUR for each card payment in the EEA, and 2.9% + 0.25 EUR for cards issued elsewhere.

## Payouts and reserve

Payouts every 2 business days. Tallowpay may hold a rolling reserve of 10% of card volume for 90 days in any month when chargebacks exceed 1% of payments.

## Chargebacks

A fee of 15 EUR for each chargeback, refunded when Fernway wins the dispute.

## Termination

Tallowpay may end the agreement or suspend payouts immediately if it suspects fraud or a breach of card scheme rules. Fernway may end the agreement with 30 days' notice.

## Data

Tallowpay acts as an independent controller for card data. Fernway never sees full card numbers.
