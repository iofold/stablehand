# Mutual non-disclosure agreement: Fernway and Brindlewood Studio

Synthetic sample for the Stablehand folder review demo. Every party in it is invented.

## 1. Purpose

The parties are discussing a redesign of the Fernway app and will share plans, usage numbers and designs.

## 2. Confidential information

Confidential information is anything one party shares with the other about its business, customers or products, in any form, except what is already public or was known to the other party before.

## 3. Residuals

Either party may use ideas, concepts and know-how that its staff keep in their unaided memory after seeing the other party's confidential information.

## 4. Term

This agreement lasts 2 years from 5 May 2026. The duty to keep information confidential continues for 2 years after it ends.

## 5. Return of information

At the end of the agreement, each party deletes or returns the other party's documents within 30 days.
