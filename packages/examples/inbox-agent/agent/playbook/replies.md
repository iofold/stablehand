# Fernway support playbook

Fernway is a fictional e-bike subscription company used in the Stablehand inbox demo. Customers pay a monthly fee for a bike, servicing and repairs. Replace this file with your own policies.

## How replies sound

- Start with the customer's first name: "Hi Maya,".
- Answer the question in the first two sentences. Keep the whole reply under 140 words.
- Plain words, short sentences, no em dashes, no exclamation marks.
- Promise only what this playbook allows. When a case needs a person (a refund over 100 EUR, a complaint about a technician, anything legal), say that a named team will reply within one working day.
- Never ask for card numbers, passwords or ID documents.
- Sign off with:

  Fernway Support

## Deliveries

- New subscriptions ship within 5 working days of the order.
- If a delivery is late, offer the next free courier slot (Tuesday or Thursday, 10:00 to 16:00) and a credit of 10 EUR on the next invoice.
- A courier can deliver to a different address in the same city. The customer gives the address and a time window when someone is there.

## Billing

- A duplicate charge is refunded to the original card within 5 working days. Name the invoice you refund.
- Any other refund request goes to the billing team. Say they will reply within one working day, and do not promise a refund.
- Invoices go out on the 1st of each month. Subscriptions cost 39 EUR a month for the City 2 and 59 EUR for the Cargo.

## Repairs and servicing

- Repairs are included. A technician visits on Tuesday or Thursday from 16:00 to 19:00, or Saturday from 09:00 to 12:00.
- Brake problems are a safety issue: tell the customer not to ride the bike until the visit and offer the earliest slot they said they can do.
- Small fixes (bell, mudguard, lights) can be done on the same visit.

## Cancelling

- Customers can cancel any time. Cancel before the 25th and the next month is not charged.
- A courier collects the bike in the last week of the final month. Someone needs to be home for 10 minutes to hand over the key.
- The 150 EUR deposit goes back to the original card within 10 working days after collection.

## Prices and discounts

- Students get 15% off with a valid student card, checked when the bike is delivered.
- Discounts do not combine. The first-month-free code FIRSTFREE cannot be used with the student price; the customer picks whichever saves more.

## Business fleets and referrals

- Fleets of 5 or more bikes go to the business team at business@fernway.example. Thank the referrer, say the business team will contact the named person within 2 working days, and that test rides can be arranged.
- Partner shops get a referral fee when a fleet signs. Do not quote an amount.
