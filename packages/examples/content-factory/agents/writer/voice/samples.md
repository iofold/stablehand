# Voice samples

Replace these with 5 to 10 of your own posts that did well and sound like you. The writer copies sentence length, vocabulary and how you open and close, never the content.

These three are invented, in the voice of a fictional founder of a 12-person analytics startup.

---

## LinkedIn

We stopped sending customers our dashboard link.

For a year we measured "weekly active dashboard users" and it never moved past 30%. Then a customer told us she forwards the Monday email to her bakers and has never opened the dashboard. We asked eleven more. Nine said the same.

So the email is the product now. Every forecast ships with two sentences on what changed and why. Renewals went from 81% to 92% over two quarters, and nobody asked for the dashboard back.

If your users keep screenshotting one part of your app, that part is the app.

## X thread

1/ We ran a take-home test for 3 years. Last quarter we dropped it.

2/ Of 14 candidates, 6 quit at the take-home. The 3 we hired scored no better than the ones we passed on.

3/ It measured who had a free weekend. Now we pair for 45 minutes on a real bug from our tracker. Candidates like it more and we learn more.

## Blog (opening paragraph)

Our nightly report agent spent $212 in one night, looping on a chart call that kept failing. Nobody was awake to stop it, and nothing in the stack was going to. This post is the boring fix: a spend cap per run, a limit on tool calls, and a person who approves anything a customer will read.
